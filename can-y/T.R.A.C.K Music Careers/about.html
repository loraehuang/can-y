Training Routes in Audio & Creative Knowledge

about track

T.R.A.C.K is a free directory for exploring training pathways across music, sound, visual arts, media, and entertainment.

what it is for

The site was built to make career research less scattered. It helps students look up jobs, read what the work involves, follow possible career steps, and find schools or training programs connected to the roles they are exploring.

what users can explore

  • Career descriptions, responsibilities, starting points, and progression steps
  • Search by job title, school, state, entry path, or career area
  • Program matches for users comparing several career interests
  • U.S. and international schools, training programs, and career-development options

access

T.R.A.C.K is free to use. It does not require an account, login, subscription, or tracking profile.
