Methodology & Sources

What T.R.A.C.K Is

T.R.A.C.K is an independent research directory for music, audio, and creative-career pathways. It began as a personal project to put jobs, schools, programs, and training routes in one place. The directory is updated as the research grows. It is a starting point for exploration, not official educational, career, legal, or financial advice. It is not a ranking, school partnership, or educational consulting service.

How Listings Are Compiled

School and program listings are built through editorial research using publicly available information, including institutional websites, accreditation databases, industry publications, and program catalogs. Listings reflect editorial judgment about which programs seem most directly relevant to each career path - they are not paid placements, sponsored listings, or official endorsements.

Program Locations and Factual Labels

T.R.A.C.K is not a ranking service. Programs are organized by location, not quality, standing, or preference. The labels shown with individual listings describe factual details available in the directory, such as institution type, credential level, delivery format, research emphasis, or language information. They are not rankings or endorsements.

  • U.S. Programs - Programs based in the United States, including degree, conservatory, technical, online, and professional training options.
  • Programs Outside the U.S. - Programs based outside the United States. Language information is identified when it is available in the listing.

Sources & Limitations

Program descriptions, highlights, and statistics are drawn from publicly available institutional sources. Where statistics appear (for example, career outcomes percentages), these are school-reported figures and approximate values - they have not been independently verified by T.R.A.C.K unless explicitly stated.

References to third-party lists, features, accreditors, or editorial mentions are provided only to indicate that a program appeared in a published external source. Unless explicitly stated and directly sourced, T.R.A.C.K does not make any claim about a program’s rank, comparative standing, endorsement, or quality based on those references.

Individual listings may reflect information gathered at different points in time and may become outdated between review cycles. The footer indicates the most recent overall review date, but individual entries may vary in recency. Program availability, tuition, accreditation status, and admission requirements change frequently. T.R.A.C.K does not guarantee the accuracy or completeness of any listing. Always rely on official institution sources before making any educational, financial, transfer, or career decision.

T.R.A.C.K's database is reviewed and updated periodically. Individual program entries may reflect information gathered at different points in time. When in doubt, verify directly with the institution.
